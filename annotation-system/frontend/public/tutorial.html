Welcome to the Group Chat Study!

You are helping create an open dataset that evaluates where external information could help group conversations.

This tutorial walks you through the study step by step.

Study Procedure Overview

The study will last ~90 minutes. There will be four stages as shown on the right.

The core question to think about throughout the study is: "What external information (e.g., a fact, a recommendation) do I wish a knowledgeable participant could have contributed to help our conversation be more productive and smooth? At what point of the conversation?"

You don't need to remember these. The annotation system will guide you through the process.

Stage 1: Conversation

The goal for the first stage is to have a real conversation with other participants. You will work with the conversation transcript in later stages of the study.

Feel free to push back, share views you feel strongly about, disagree productively. You are not evaluated on what you say in the conversation.

The researcher will end the conversation after ~25 minutes.

Stage 2: Create Messages

Think, what external information (e.g., a fact, a recommendation) could have helped the conversation you just had?

Review your transcript from Stage 1 and create inserted messages that contain those information.

Stage 3: Annotate

In this stage, you will see the messages created by your group mates and determine where they should go.

For each message, either:

  • Select the placement window and rate importance
  • Mark "Should not appear" if you think it wouldn't be useful at any point

Use Prev/Next to navigate. Click "Save Annotation" to save each message.

Ranking

After annotating all messages, rank them: considering all messages (including your own), which would be most valuable to the group? Drag to reorder.

Messages marked "should not appear" are separated at the bottom. You can reorder within each section but not across them.

Click "Submit ranking" when done. You'll wait for other group members to finish before Stage 4.

Stage 4: Review & Revise

Annotation Review

In this last stage, you will see how others in your group judged the same messages. You'll see colored overlays showing where other participants placed each message.

For each message, decide whether to revise your placement window, best turn, importance, or switch to "Should not appear."

After reviewing all messages, click "Proceed to Ranking Review."

Ranking Review

You'll see your ranking alongside your peers' rankings. Each card shows color-coded badges indicating how everyone ranked it (legend at top).

Drag to reorder if you find others' rankings compelling. When satisfied, click "Save ranking and complete review."

You'll then see a summary of your decisions while waiting for other group members to finish.

What Happens After

Once all group members complete Stage 4, your session is done. Your annotations and rankings are saved automatically.

Congratulations! Your contributions become part of an open dataset to evaluate AI agents.

We need a lot of participants (120)! Please share the call for participation with friends and colleagues.
